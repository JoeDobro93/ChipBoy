# Captures

Put recordings here. `.wav` files and `*_measured.json` are git-ignored — recordings are
large and belong in your own storage, not in the repository.

Suggested naming, matching `docs/CAPTURE_GUIDE.md`:

```
loopback_dmg.wav        loopback_cgb.wav
dmg_vol_max.wav         cgb_vol_max.wav
dmg_vol_mid.wav         cgb_vol_mid.wav
dmg_noise_gamecart.wav
```

The `*_measured.json` files the analyser writes are the evidence behind every constant in
`docs/HARDWARE_REFERENCE.md` §12 — keep those somewhere durable even though they are not
tracked here.
